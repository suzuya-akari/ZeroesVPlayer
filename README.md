# -1-
ZeroesVPlayer is a Metal Gear Solid V Ground Zeroed themed Music Player.
It includes full Ground Zeroes's UI, Cassette and can play your own music too you, support Lossless and MP3. You can get a MP3, Lossless from the internet.

You can triple tap into the background and it will automatically go fullscreen. You can install the PWA then uploads your audio files locally, no internet, nothing. Full offline.
